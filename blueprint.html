Micro-interactions & treatments

  • Ember pulse — a live orange dot next to today’s date and “open now” states; the kitchen is on.
  • Market ticker — full-bleed ember marquee of today’s produce and its origin (Tara, Grocka, Sjenica). Fed from the same menu data.
  • Flickering full stop in the logo and hero — subtle flame flicker, 3s loop.
  • Chalkboard logic — sold-out items fade and strike through instead of disappearing (scarcity = proof of freshness).
  • Hard edges — no rounded corners, no soft gradients; 1px rules and dotted price leaders like a handwritten menu.
  • Photography — handheld, high-contrast, steam and smoke; hands, knives, crates. Never plated-on-white stock.
  • Press feedback — card lift on hover, accent fill on selected slot/day, instant inline confirmations.

03 · Dynamic menu & QR

Chef updates in 30 seconds

  • Password-protected “Režim kuhinje” on the phone (try it in the prototype): toggle Nestalo, ±50 RSD, Novo danas, add dish.
  • Each item: name, ingredients/origin, price, category, tags (vege, alergeni 1–14 per EU list), location.
  • “Ažurirano u 11:42” stamp builds trust that the menu is real today.
  • Micro-season line and ticker pull from the same data — one edit, updated everywhere.
  • Optional auto-post of the daily menu as an Instagram story card.

QR strategy

  • One printed code per table: koordinata.rs/m/zemun/4 — opens straight to today’s menu, no app, no PDF.
  • Table ID enables “call the waiter” and future pay-at-table without reprinting.
  • Engraved wood/steel tags at the market (weather-proof); small linen cards in the bistro.
  • Page under 100 KB, cached offline — market Wi-Fi is unreliable.
  • Large type, dark mode by default (sunlight + candlelight), allergens one tap away.

04 · Build & launch

  • Front-end: static site (Astro or plain HTML), the prototype’s components ported 1:1; mobile-first, Lighthouse 95+.
  • Menu data: headless CMS (Sanity / Directus) or a Google Sheet → JSON; webhooks rebuild the menu in seconds.
  • Booking: small API (Supabase/Postgres) with slots + capacity tables, SMS via Infobip; or a white-labelled booking service if speed matters more than control.
  • SEO: Restaurant schema per location, Google Business Profile booking link, Serbian primary, English mirror.
  • Phase 1 (3 wks): site + live menu + QR. Phase 2 (3 wks): booking + SMS + staff view.
